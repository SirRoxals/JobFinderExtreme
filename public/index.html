Across companies. Automatically.

The broad catalogue searches ATS platforms and job boards automatically, alongside direct Indeed search, LinkedIn guest search and specialist indexes. These are actual fetched results, checked locally. This panel shows candidate counts, sources, delays, and failures.

Coverage has limits.

No public index covers every job. A completed search means the selected queries finished within their page limits, not that the entire internet was searched. Deep search reads more pages.

LinkedIn imports only postings whose full page can be read and whose US/remote eligibility can be established. A blocked guest search is shown as a source failure. The external board shortcuts open separately and are never counted as imported results.
